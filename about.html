How it started

It started with one car. It hasn't stopped since.

I'm Griffin. I rebuilt my grandfather's '89 Mustang in college — that's the silhouette in our logo — and the obsession that came with it never really left. While I was finishing my automotive technology degree in 2023, I started detailing cars on the side. After graduating, I took a private training course in paint correction and ceramic coating, brought my partner Grayson in, and opened the shop.

We're not a dealership and we're not a chain. We're the underdogs, and that's the way we like it. We answer our own phones, we put our own hands on every car, and we'd rather make a client for life than make a quick sale.

What we believe

Four things we won't compromise on.

No surprises, no rush jobs, no upsells. Every car we touch gets the same standard — ours.

Honesty

If your paint doesn't need a full correction, we'll tell you. If a service isn't right for how you drive, we'll talk you out of it. We'd rather lose a sale than burn trust.

Why CCI

We use the coatings we'd put on our own cars.

When you spend years studying how paint reacts to chemistry, you stop trusting marketing copy. We chose Ceramic Coating Inc (CCI) after testing their products against the alternatives ourselves — on our cars, in our shop, through Illinois winters and Midwest summers.

The result: a coating partner we're proud to be Multi-Certified with, a warranty we can stand behind, and a product line we install with confidence. You don't just get a coating. You get the right coating for your vehicle, applied by people trained on the exact chemistry going on your paint.

How we work

From first call to handoff.

  1. 01

    Honest Assessment

    You send a few photos or stop by. We look at the paint, talk through your goals, and tell you exactly what your car needs — even when that's less than you expected.

  2. 02

    Clear, Written Quote

    A written estimate broken down by service. No hidden fees, no “we found something” mid-job phone calls. The number we quote is the number you pay.
