Our story

Not a chain. Not a dealership. Just us.

O'Connor Detailing is a small, independently-owned shop in Peoria Heights — just me and my partner Grayson, both 24, both fully in on this. I started detailing on the side while studying automotive tech in college. Upon graduating in May of 2023, I took a private training course in paint correction and ceramic coating and went all-in.

We're not a dealership. We're not a corporate chain. We're the underdogs, and that's the way we like it. It means we answer our own phones, we know every car and face that comes through the door, and we care more about our clients succeeding and benefiting from our services than we do about making a dollar.

When you book with us, your car isn't getting handed off to a rotation of techs. It's the two of us, every time. That's how we keep the work consistent — and how we keep the bar high.

Mission & vision

Why we open the shop every morning.

A small business with a clear purpose — and a clear picture of where we're headed.

To raise the standard of vehicle care in central Illinois — one car, one honest conversation at a time. We exist to give Peoria drivers a place where the quote is fair, the prep is thorough, and the work outlasts the warranty.

To be the first name central Illinois drivers think of when they want their vehicle protected — and to set the bar for what an independent ceramic coating shop looks like, without ever losing the small-shop care that got us here.

How we work

From first call to handoff.

No surprises. No upsells you don't need. Here's exactly what to expect when you bring us a vehicle.

Start a quote →
1 · Honest assessment+

You send us a few photos or stop by. We look at the paint, talk through your goals, and tell you exactly what your car actually needs — even when that's less than you expected.

2 · Clear, written quote+

You get a written estimate broken down by service. No hidden fees, no "we found something" mid-job phone calls. The number we quote is the number you pay.

3 · The work, in our shop+

Decontamination, correction, coating — done in our climate-controlled shop, hands-on by Griffin and Grayson. We send progress photos along the way so you're never wondering.

4 · Handoff & aftercare+

We walk you through how to wash and care for the coating so it lasts. You leave with a maintenance plan, our number, and a car that looks better than the day you bought it.

What we stand for

Three things we won't compromise on.

Honesty. If your paint doesn't need a full correction, we'll tell you. If a service isn't right for your driving, we'll talk you out of it. We'd rather earn a customer for life than upsell once.

Craft. Coatings are only as good as the prep. We don't cut prep corners — ever. That's why our work holds up years later when other shops' work doesn't.

Care. No dings, no swirl marks from a careless wash mitt, no second-shift rush jobs. We treat every car like our own — because that's the only way we know how to work.
